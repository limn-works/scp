**STEP 4 BLOCKER (escalated, did NOT stub):** SyncPhaseDriver CANNOT be implemented fully-functional over &ActorDeps. The actor's `ContextTransportProvider` (deps.transport) is SEND-ONLY (send_message/send_to_routing_id/publish_*) — it has NO message-retrieval (subscribe/query with `since`). Buffered-message retrieval lives in the FFI/SDK relay-client layer (context_subscribe in napi/pyo3 drives scp-transport NativeRelayAdapter::subscribe/query directly); the actor only RECEIVES pushed msgs via deliver_incoming. So `relay_catch_up` (Phase 1) + `epoch_reconciliation`/`sender_key_reacquire`/`observed_target_epoch`/`blob_ttl_secs` (all consume BufferedMessage) have no ActorDeps provider. Also `local_epoch` (epoch is in PerContextState.epoch.mls_epoch, NOT reachable from &ActorDeps) and `mls_update` (no public MLS-commit-issuance on MlsCryptoProvider). Confirmed: ReconnectionCoordinator::plan doc explicitly says "transport layer is injected at the SDK level, not in scp-core" — the tier engines (SyncPhaseDriver, ResetTransport) were DESIGNED to be driven at the SDK level. Wiring the driver over ActorDeps requires either (a) adding retrieve_buffered(ctx,since) to ContextTransportProvider + impl in NativeRelayAdapter + all impls (large cross-layer surface), or (b) driving the coordinator at the FFI layer where TransportManager lives, OR (c) the driver taking &mut PerContextState + a new retrieval dep. This is a genuine architectural decision needing human direction, NOT a stub.
