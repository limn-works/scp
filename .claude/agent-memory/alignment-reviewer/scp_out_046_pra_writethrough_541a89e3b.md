**How to apply (the headline finding — .docs/specs/06-cross-context-communication.md:366):** The new clause "This durable capture is **write-through with persist-before-forward ordering** (ADR-061): no chunk is forwarded to the caller before its frontier advance is durable, so the delivered set is always a subset of the sealed prefix — the caller is never over-charged and the operator never leaks revenue" is NOT backed by ADR-061 and partially CONTRADICTS it:
- ADR-061:39 says the durable capture "never gates caller-forward latency" (forward-as-produced, two parallel sinks) — persist-before-forward is exactly a gate, so the clause contradicts :39.
- ADR-061:62 explicitly TOLERATES a "coalesce crash" (credit `commit_class_s_keep` never un-consumed on a coalesce crash); ADR-061:48 seals the "last durable prefix" which can be SHORTER than what was delivered.
- Real invariant (ADR-061 seal-prefix + §5.4.5:558/:566): billing = billed_count over the sealed/signed manifest of VALIDLY DELIVERED chunks; on a coalesce crash sealed ⊆ delivered, so caller is never over-charged (billed ≤ delivered) and the operator MAY leak revenue on the lost tail (tolerated). The clause asserts the OPPOSITE direction (delivered ⊆ sealed, operator never leaks).
- The clause is also internally incoherent: delivered ⊆ sealed (persist-before-forward) means billing over `sealed` can EXCEED `delivered` → caller CAN be over-charged for a persisted-but-undelivered tail — the reverse of what it claims. "never over-charged AND never leaks" requires delivered==sealed exactly (atomic forward+persist), unachievable across a crash with either fsync cadence. And "fsync coalesced is an implementation choice" is incompatible with strict per-chunk persist-before-forward.
- LEGIT parts of the clause: "write-through" (ADR-061:63 two-sinks), and "recovery seals the last durable prefix and settles escrow at its billed_count" (ADR-061:48) are faithful.
- FIX (either): (a) reword to the ADR's actual invariant — forward-as-produced/never-gating, caller-never-over-charged via sealed⊆delivered, coalesce-crash may seal a shorter prefix (tolerated operator revenue leak on the lost tail); OR (b) if persist-before-forward is genuinely desired, AMEND ADR-061 FIRST (artifact-flow), then flow down. As written it cites "(ADR-061)" for a guarantee the ADR does not make = phantom provenance.
